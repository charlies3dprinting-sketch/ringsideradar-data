Ringside Radar

Delete your account

Ringside Radar, made by Charlie Cook, lets you delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Ringside Radar and go to the Me tab (in version 1.1: the Profile tab).
  2. Tap Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete for good.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can’t open the app, email charlies3dprinting@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Ringside Radar account”, from the email address you signed in with. We’ll delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your login and email address
  • Your username and leaderboard entry (points and show count)
  • Your profile photo, bio and home area, and the backup of your passport stamps
  • Fans you follow and block, your group memberships (and groups you created), and your Going/Interested marks
  • Your match ratings, reviews, comments, fan-poll ballot, reports and corrections
  • Your wrestler or promoter account and photos you uploaded
  • The match results you sent

Nothing is kept after deletion. Results that other fans also reported can stay in the app as fan-confirmed results, with no link to you. Shows and results a verified promoter posted stay listed without your account attached. Settings saved on your phone stay there; delete the app or use Me → Settings → Reset app data to remove them.
