Community Guidelines

Effective October 4, 2026

Ringside Radar is for fans, wrestlers and promoters who love wrestling. Talk about the show, not about tearing people down.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech or slurs, including toward wrestlers, promoters and other fans.
  • No sexual content, and nothing involving minors.
  • Don’t share anyone’s private information (addresses, phone numbers, real names they keep private).

Keep it real

  • Only report results you actually saw, and only list shows, cards and titles that are real.
  • Don’t impersonate a wrestler, promotion or another fan.
  • No spam, scams, ticket resale schemes or unrelated advertising.

Spoilers

Results are part of the app, but be kind in comments: give a heads-up before spoiling a show that just happened. Fans can turn on spoiler mode in Me → Settings.

Photos and posters

Only upload images you took or have permission to use. No graphic violence beyond normal wrestling photography.

Reporting and enforcement

Use Report on comments, reviews, groups and profiles, or block someone so you don’t see them. We review reports and may hide content, remove listings, or suspend accounts. Repeated or serious violations lead to removal.

See also the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
