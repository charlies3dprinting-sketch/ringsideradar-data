Ringside Radar

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

The short version: You can use Ringside Radar without an account. If you choose to sign in, we keep your email address (only to sign you in), the username you pick, your passport points and the match results you send. Your location never leaves your phone. No ads, no analytics, and we don’t sell or share your information. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Ringside Radar is a pro wrestling app for finding shows, following wrestlers and promotions, and tracking rankings. It is made by Charlie Cook (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your phone’s location while you are using it. It uses that location for two things:

  • showing wrestling shows near you and how far away they are, and
  • confirming you are inside the venue when you check in to a show for a passport stamp.

Your location is used only on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the app never reads your location in the background. You can say no or turn location off at any time in your phone’s settings and pick a city by hand instead.

Accounts (optional)

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, we collect and store:

  • Your email address, used only to send you sign-in codes. It is never shown to other fans and never used for marketing.
  • Your username, which you choose. It is public: other fans see it on the passport leaderboard and it may appear next to results you report.
  • Your passport totals (points and number of shows), shown on the public leaderboard. Your individual stamps, follows and settings stay on your phone.
  • Match results you send from shows you checked in to: the show, the wrestlers in the match, who won, and whether it was a title match. When at least three fans report the same result it is shown in the app as fan-confirmed, without names attached.

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States, and is sent over encrypted connections. Sign-in emails are sent through Google’s Gmail service from our support address.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your login, email address, username, leaderboard entry and the results you sent. Fan-confirmed results that other fans also reported stay, with no link to you. If you can’t get into the app, email charlies3dprinting@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account within 30 days. More details: delete your account.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves your settings on your phone so they are there next time: the wrestlers and promotions you follow, your alert settings, your city and show radius, your passport stamps and points, your app theme, and any claim you start. This information is not uploaded, except your passport totals if you sign in (see above). Deleting the app deletes it.

Show data and photos

To stay up to date, the app downloads a public data file of shows, results and rankings from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Wrestler photos are free-licensed images from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each wrestler’s page, and are loaded from our site on GitHub Pages (github.io). Like any website, GitHub receives your phone’s IP address when these files are downloaded; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The app does not send any of your information with these requests.

When you email us

If you claim a wrestler or promotion profile, ask us to add a promotion, or tap “Contact us”, the app opens your own email app with a message to charlies3dprinting@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. A claim email includes the profile you are claiming, the social media account you named, a verification code, and for promotions your role and contact email. We use it only to verify the claim and reply to you, and we delete it on request.

Ticket and other links

Ticket buttons and other links open websites run by promotions and ticket sellers. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking IDs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don’t sell or share your information, and we don’t use it for anything except running the features described here.

Children

Ringside Radar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can use the app without signing in, turn off location access in your phone’s settings, change your username or delete your account from Profile → Account, reset the app’s saved data from the Profile tab, or delete the app. To ask about your data, write to charlies3dprinting@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
