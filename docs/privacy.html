RingsideRadar

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

The short version: RingsideRadar has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your location, follows, alerts and passport stamps stay on your phone. We only hear from you if you choose to email us.

RingsideRadar is a pro wrestling app for finding shows, following wrestlers and promotions, and tracking rankings. It is made by Charlie Cook (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your phone’s location while you are using it. It uses that location for two things:

  • showing wrestling shows near you and how far away they are, and
  • confirming you are inside the venue when you check in to a show for a passport stamp.

Your location is used only on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the app never reads your location in the background. You can say no or turn location off at any time in your phone’s settings and pick a city by hand instead.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves your settings on your phone so they are there next time: the wrestlers and promotions you follow, your alert settings, your city and show radius, your passport stamps and points, your display name, your app theme, and any claim you start. This information is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Show and ranking data

To stay up to date, the app downloads a public data file of shows, results and rankings from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any website, GitHub receives your phone’s IP address when this file is downloaded; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The app does not send any of your information with this request.

When you email us

If you claim a wrestler or promotion profile, ask us to add a promotion, or tap “Contact us”, the app opens your own email app with a message to charlies3dprinting@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. A claim email includes the profile you are claiming, the social media account you named, a verification code, and for promotions your role and contact email. We use it only to verify the claim and reply to you, and we delete it on request.

Ticket and other links

Ticket buttons and other links open websites run by promotions and ticket sellers. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or logins.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking IDs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don’t sell or share your information.

Children

RingsideRadar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off location access in your phone’s settings, reset the app’s saved data from the Profile tab, or delete the app. To ask about or delete any email you sent us, write to charlies3dprinting@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a future version adds accounts or shared features, we’ll describe them here before they launch.
